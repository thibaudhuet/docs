## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I upgrade or downgrade at any time?">
    Yes. You can upgrade immediately and the new limits apply instantly. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I exceed my daily limit?">
    Requests beyond your daily limit return a `429 Too Many Requests` error. Your quota resets at midnight UTC. You can upgrade your plan at any time to increase your limits — see [Rate Limits](/rate-limits) for retry guidance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial for the Growth plan?">
    Yes — all new accounts start with a **14-day free trial** of the Growth plan, no credit card required. After the trial, you'll automatically drop to the Starter (Free) plan unless you upgrade.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What counts as a 'request'?">
    Each HTTP call to an Insights API endpoint counts as one request, regardless of how many scenarios you pass or how large the response is.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do you offer academic or startup discounts?">
    Yes. [Contact us](mailto:sales@simcel.io) with proof of eligibility and we'll apply a discount to the Growth plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What payment methods do you accept?">
    We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) and bank transfer for annual Enterprise contracts.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
